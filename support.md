## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I add a transaction?**
Tap the **+** button on the Transactions screen, fill in the amount, description, and date, then save.

**How do I create a category?**
Go to the Categories tab and tap **+**. Give it a name, pick a colour, and optionally set a monthly budget limit.

**Can I edit or delete a transaction?**
Yes — tap any transaction to open it, then use the edit or delete action.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Your financial data is stored on your personal account and is never sold or shared. See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy) for full details.

**How do I sign out?**
Open the Settings tab and tap **Sign Out**.
