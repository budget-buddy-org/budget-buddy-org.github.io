Budget Buddy is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

## Information We Collect

**Financial data you enter** — transactions, categories, and budget amounts — is stored on your personal account and is never sold or shared with third parties.

**Account information** — when you sign in, we store a secure identifier linked to your authentication provider. We do not store your password.

**Diagnostic information** — the app may collect anonymous crash reports and usage diagnostics to help us fix bugs and improve stability. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and operate the Budget Buddy service
- To improve app performance and fix issues
- To respond to support requests you initiate

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

## Third-Party Services

Budget Buddy may use the following third-party services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

- **Apple** — for authentication (Sign in with Apple), crash reporting (if enabled), and App Store distribution
- **Analytics and crash reporting providers** — anonymous technical data only

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely. We use industry-standard measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

## Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the support address below.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us via the [Support](./support) page.
